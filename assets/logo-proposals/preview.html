sparq logo — proposals

Five distinct minimal/geometric combination marks for sparq, on the privacy-first teal brand (light #1c7f86 / dark #4cc4cc) with an amber energy accent. Each SVG is light/dark aware via prefers-color-scheme — toggle the button to preview dark mode. The current assets/logo.svg is unchanged; these are proposals.

Recommended: Concept 2 (graph-as-“q”) as the primary combination mark, with the Concept 3 shield as the favicon / app-icon where a bold silhouette matters most at 16–32 px.

Combination marks (icon + wordmark)

1 — Refined graph + spark Evolves the current logo: even nodes, the apex node is the amber spark (no longer floating). Safest, most familiar.
2 — Graph forms a “q” (query) A ring of nodes is the bowl of a lowercase q; one edge drops to an amber terminus node as the tail. Distinctive, on-brand for SPARQ.
3 — Privacy / proof shield RDF triple knocked out of an escutcheon shield; amber = the verified node. Signals the ZK / MPC differentiator. Best bold silhouette.
4 — Spark is the edge (speed) The amber lightning bolt becomes the connecting edge traversing three nodes — a fast query racing through the store.
5 — Abstract triple Subject (amber circle) → predicate (edges) → object (diamond): the atomic RDF triple as a confident standalone mark.
